Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding our clinical procedures, hearing aid fittings, and speech therapy.

Who conducts the hearing tests and speech therapy sessions?

All evaluations, device programming, and speech therapy sessions are conducted exclusively by Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI) registered Audiologists and Speech-Language Pathologists with extensive clinical experience.

Do you offer live trials for digital hearing aids before purchasing?

Yes! We provide live hearing aid trials for leading global manufacturers—including Phonak, Oticon, Signia, GN ReSound, and Rexton. The devices are programmed to match your unique audiogram during your visit so you can experience real-world clarity.

How long does a complete computerized hearing evaluation take?

A thorough diagnostic session—including Pure Tone Audiometry (PTA), Speech Audiometry, and Immittance/Tympanometry testing—takes approximately 30 to 45 minutes in our calibrated soundproof testing booth.

At what age can a child begin speech therapy?

Early intervention is vital for speech and language development. Children can be evaluated for speech delays or developmental milestone gaps as early as 18 months of age.

Is an appointment required before visiting the Vatakara center?

We recommend scheduling an appointment in advance via call or WhatsApp (+91 8921015858) to ensure zero waiting time and dedicated individual attention from our clinical team.
